## Notes on the sources
- The faculty's role ↔ artifact match key pairs **Scrum Master ↔ Sprint Backlog**; the Scrum Guide gives the sprint backlog to the developers. Use the faculty pairing in this course's exam (flagged in Ch 3 §6).
- The faculty slide gives **flow efficiency = cycle time ÷ lead time**; many books define it as active time ÷ lead time (flagged in Ch 8 §5).
- The retrospective is taught as Derby & Larsen's four core stages; the slide adds a fifth "close the retrospective" step. Write all five and say the first four are the core progression.
- No slides were uploaded for units 4, 6 and 8 (initiation, planning detail, MSP cases); that content is marked 📗.
